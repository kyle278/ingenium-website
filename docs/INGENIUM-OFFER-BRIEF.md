## 2. Dream outcome

**In their words:** "When someone hears about us, I want them to look us up, trust what they see and know how to get in touch. I don’t want another job on my list."

**Status version:** The business looks as dependable online as it is on a job. The owner feels comfortable sharing a link with a referral instead of explaining why the website is missing or out of date.

The website offers support that outcome. They do not promise a number of calls, rankings, bookings or revenue. The CRM offers address the separate problem of following an enquiry through to the next action.
