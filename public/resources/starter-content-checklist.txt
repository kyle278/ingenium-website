STARTER WEBSITE: YOUR CONTENT CHECKLIST

Send this material once your scope is agreed. The target five-working-day delivery period starts when your deposit, required content and access have been received.

BUSINESS
Trading name, contact name, business phone, enquiry email, service area, opening hours and relevant registration details.
Tell us the main thing you want a visitor to do: call or send an enquiry.

YOUR FIVE SECTIONS
1. Introduction: what you do, who you help and where you work.
2. Services: concise descriptions of the agreed services.
3. About: your experience and the facts customers should know.
4. Work or testimonials: photographs you can use and feedback you have permission to publish.
5. Contact: the agreed details and form questions.

BRAND AND ACCESS
Logo, colours, approved images and your current domain provider.
Arrange access securely. Do not send passwords in this checklist.
Domain registration/renewal and copywriting from scratch are separate.

APPROVAL
Choose one person to gather feedback.
Send one consolidated round of revisions within scope.
Check the business details, spelling, service area, claims and images before approval.
