# Irish package validation and sales strategy
Prepared for Ingenium Consulting • 29 October 2026
Scope: Irish small businesses and startups; desk research, competitive positioning and website conversion strategy.

## The decision

Keep the three-package ladder. Make Business Growth the main commercial offer. Sell Starter Website as a quick, tightly scoped credibility project, and sell CRM & Automation through discovery around a costly operational problem.

The strongest initial audience is **quote-led service businesses**: landscaping and outdoor services, trades and contractors, cleaning and property services, and selected professional services. “Irish SMEs and startups” is a market description, but too broad to be a persuasive sales message.

This research supports the existence of the problems and the plausibility of the prices. It does **not** establish willingness to pay Ingenium, delivery profitability, close rates, retention or product-market fit. Those require real customer conversations, deposits, delivery logs and subscription cohorts. Competitor advertised prices show alternatives, not their sales volumes or profitability.

The user's later commercial clarification is authoritative: Ingenium is not VAT registered, so current customer copy says **No VAT charged**. Business Growth and CRM & Automation are **indicative starting prices**, subject to scope.

### Starter verdict

**Valid entry offer; vulnerable to commoditisation.** €495 promotional setup is credible in the sampled market, but is not a defensible differentiator. The standard €795 needs to be sold through design quality, useful structure, real work examples, reliable delivery and a clear handover.

The €50/month service must be described accurately: hosting, SSL, technical maintenance, monitoring and support. Do not imply that it includes ongoing content work, Google rankings, domain registration or email hosting. Competitors offering these create a possible objection. Either preserve the limited scope and explain the service well, or price a defined content allowance after costing it. Do not quietly add unlimited changes.

Starter's first-year cash commitment is €1,095 during the promotion or €1,395 at standard setup, assuming twelve €50 subscription months and no free-hosting commitment. These are arithmetic comparisons, not minimum terms.

### Business Growth verdict

**Best candidate for the main offer.** Sell one connected enquiry journey rather than a bigger website. The buyer should see their website enquiry become a record, a visible opportunity and an acknowledgement.

Its indicative first-year cost is from €2,688: €1,500 + 12 × €99. The incremental first-year cost over standard Starter is €1,293. A sales conversation should compare that increment with the customer's own cost of duplicated administration and stalled enquiries, not assert that one new customer will pay for it.

At €1,500, the combination of five pages, CRM, workflow, reporting and training could be underpriced if projects drift. Keep one pipeline and one workflow. Source copy and imagery from the client. Define user, email, execution and support allowances. Measure build time before fixing the price.

### CRM & Automation verdict

**Valid higher-value service, conditional on discovery and adoption.** “From €3,000” fits a base implementation better than a fixed promise to replace a business's entire operating stack. The initial year is from €5,388: €3,000 + 12 × €199.

Make the offer about one clearly mapped process. The website is separate. One external integration is subject to feasibility, not “anything connects.” Require a cleaned dataset and agree record limits before committing.

Do not sell to a company merely because it has spreadsheets. Establish frequency, handovers, consequences, an internal owner and willingness to change the process. A simple spreadsheet may be the appropriate tool for a low-volume business.

## 3. Refined ideal customer list

The ranking below is a strategic judgement based on process fit, not a measured conversion league table.

| Segment | Best initial path | Trigger to qualify | Words to use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Landscaping, paving, fencing, garden projects | Business Growth | Regular site visits and quotes; owner cannot see which quotes need attention. | “See who needs a quote, who needs a reply and what happens next.” |
| Cleaning and property services | Business Growth, then CRM | Mixed residential/commercial enquiries, multiple staff or a growing quote list. | “Keep new enquiries and open quotes in one place.” |
| Specialist trades and established contractors | Business Growth / CRM | Several people hand enquiries over; customer information is scattered. | “Keep the next action with the customer, even when the team is busy.” |
| Quote-led professional services and consultancies | Business Growth / CRM | Multi-step proposals, repeat follow-ups and an internal process owner. | “A shared view of enquiries, proposals and next actions.” |
| Sole traders and newly trading local services | Starter | Referrals already arrive, but prospects cannot easily check services or work. | “Give referrals somewhere to check your work and get in touch.” |
| Salons, beauty and appointment-led wellness | Starter, or separately scoped booking work | Existing booking software; website needs to present services and link to bookings. | “Help people choose your services and find your booking link.” |
| Retail, hospitality and ecommerce | Separate discovery | Product catalogue, payments, reservations or stock are the actual need. | “Let us scope the buying or booking journey first.” |
| Pre-revenue software startups | Selective discovery | Need investor/product validation, a product build or a complex funnel. | Avoid treating a brochure website as the solution to product-market fit. |

Fresha and Phorest already combine booking, client management, reminders and related salon workflows. That makes a generic replacement CRM a harder first sale to a salon than a website that complements its existing system. Keep healthcare-sensitive workflows out of a standard sales pipeline demonstration. [Fresha](https://www.fresha.com/pricing), [Phorest](https://www.phorest.com/Features/).

Geography: start with existing relationships and the Carlow/South East network as a practical sales hypothesis. The public site can serve Ireland nationally. Do not invent a local market share or imply a requirement to be in Carlow.

## 4. How to sell each offer from different angles

| Buying angle | Starter | Business Growth | CRM & Automation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Financial | A clear setup price and ongoing technical cost. | A defined starting cost for a website and its first enquiry workflow. | Cost a specific process problem with the customer's numbers. |
| Emotional | Pride and confidence when somebody looks the business up. | Relief from wondering whether someone has replied. | Less dependence on one person's memory. |
| Time | Five-working-day target once inputs are ready. | One implementation covers website and enquiry handling together. | Remove agreed repetitive steps; measure before and after. |
| Effort | Content checklist, done-for-you build, one revision round. | Pre-agreed pipeline, response template and practical training. | Process map, cleaned import, ownership and acceptance checks. |
| Risk | Review agreed site before final balance/launch. | Demonstrate and test the whole form-to-CRM journey. | Phased payments, documented acceptance and the existing 14-day defect warranty. |

Do not stack every angle into the headline. Use the problem that the prospect actually described, then show the mechanism and scope.

Suggested opening questions:
- Starter: “When somebody recommends you, where do they go to check your services?”
- Growth: “Show me what happened to the last enquiry that needed a quote.”
- CRM: “Which repeated task needs the same information copied into more than one place?”

Best demo for Growth: use an explicitly labelled example, submit a test enquiry in a safe demo environment, show the contact, lead, owner, stage and acknowledgement. Explain what remains manual. Never imply WhatsApp/Facebook capture is included.

Best demo for CRM: map one real current process with sample data, agree a measurable baseline, then demonstrate the proposed replacement. Do not claim a percentage productivity gain before measuring it.

## 5. Hormozi application: strengthen value without inflating scope

1. **Dream outcome:** credibility; organised enquiries; operational control.
2. **Likelihood:** relevant real work, specific scope, visible demonstration, acceptance checks and training.
3. **Time delay:** targets with clear start conditions and an early usable process.
4. **Effort:** checklists, configuration and explicit client responsibilities.
5. **Bonuses:** reusable content, launch, response and workflow resources. These are supplied as actual downloadable templates.
6. **Urgency:** one genuine, enforced Starter price expiry.
7. **Risk reversal:** milestone acceptance for delivered functionality. The 14-day CRM warranty remains precise.
8. **Margin:** solve objections with reusable material before adding labour-heavy services.

A “€5,000 value stack” without standalone sales evidence is not substantiated. Neither is a “10× return” based on guessed job values. These are excluded from public copy. A service-credit delivery guarantee could be tested later, but it is not published or assumed affordable.

## 6. Funding: useful context, unsuitable as the main hook

The Grow Digital Voucher supports eligible new off-the-shelf software and related training/configuration. The official guidance describes 50% support, up to €5,000, with eligibility conditions, including trading history and a prior Digital for Business project. Bespoke website development and custom software are excluded; configuration/training has a proportion cap.

Do not advertise “get this website half funded.” Do not assume Ingenium Portal, supplier status, each implementation cost or a pre-launch startup is eligible. A grant-dependent prospect must consult its LEO before committing expenditure or beginning work. Provide itemised quotes separating software, configuration, training, bespoke work and other services.

Official references: [scheme](https://www.localenterprise.ie/growdigital/what-is-the-grow-digital-voucher-/), [eligibility and costs](https://www.localenterprise.ie/portal/growdigital/who-is-eligible-/), [Digital for Business overview](https://www.localenterprise.ie/dlr/financial-supports/competitiveness-supports/overview.html), [Department digital supports](https://www.enterprise.gov.ie/ga/a-ndeanaimid/tacaiochtai-dfhiontair-bheaga-agus-mheanmheide/digital-transformation/).

## 7. Homepage and conversion architecture

**Positioning:** Business growth and automation for Irish businesses.
**Headline:** Get online. Follow up. Get time back.

Three visible self-selection paths:
- “I need a professional website.” → Starter Website.
- “I need a website and better follow-up.” → Business Growth.
- “I need less admin and clearer systems.” → CRM & Automation.

Each path has its own page, problem framing, scope, price, objections and package-specific enquiry CTA. Business Growth receives a restrained visual emphasis. The comparison page shows differences in scope rather than a wall of ticks.

Homepage order: Starter offer → positioning and three paths → reassurance → package cards → service-business situations → real current work → process → team → FAQs → help choosing.

Retain real work, but distinguish a visual website example from a measured CRM outcome. Do not label portfolio screenshots as evidence of a revenue increase. Keep the existing visibility check as a secondary resource, not a fourth equal hero conversion.

## 8. The genuine seven-day promotion

Fixed campaign start: 29 October 2026 at 07:02:20 UTC.
Fixed deadline: **5 November 2026 at 07:02:20 Irish time**.
Ireland is on UTC on both dates. This is exactly seven days.

The site shows days, hours, minutes and seconds. It does not create a new period per visitor. All price calculations use the same campaign. At expiry, Starter setup is €795, savings disappear, and the banner states the promotion ended.

Offer rule chosen for conversion clarity: an eligible Starter quote request **received through the website before the deadline** keeps the €495 setup price, subject to agreeing the Starter scope. Requesting a quote does not require a deposit. An unsent form or external calendar booking alone does not reserve it. The server records receipt time and canonical pricing in the enquiry context. This avoids requiring a sales call and deposit to finish before an early-morning expiry.

The €795 is the user-supplied standard price. Keep evidence supporting that standard price; do not create a different fake anchor. No seat scarcity is asserted.

The CCPC's specific 30-day prior-price rules are stated as applying to goods, with services excluded. Do not misstate that rule as a direct website-service requirement. The commercial principle here remains simple: a real standard price and a real expiry. [CCPC guidance](https://www.ccpc.ie/business/help-for-business/guidelines-for-business/price-reductions/).

## 9. What validates the business next

Desk research is the first filter. Actual validation should record:
- Which segment, problem and package the prospect selects.
- Qualified enquiries, calls held, proposals, deposits and cash collected by package/source.
- Why a prospect declines: trust, fit, price, monthly cost, timing or lack of need.
- Actual build, revision, training and support time.
- Subscription start date, payment collection, platform costs, usage and support burden.
- Whether the client uses the pipeline and workflows after handover.

Suggested initial research cohort: **[assumption: 12 conversations]**, divided across the three initial quote-led segments. This is a qualitative learning target, not statistical significance. Ask about past behaviour before describing the package. Record a deposit as stronger evidence than enthusiasm.

Do not launch a paid acquisition budget without knowing package contribution margin and an affordable customer acquisition cost. Do not present the user's illustrative sales mix as a forecast.

## 10. Source quality and unresolved questions

Reviewed evidence spans official Irish statistics, Irish business research, official funding guidance, service providers' own pricing, CRM vendors and booking-platform documentation. Search-only snippets are treated as advertised claims rather than independently verified customer outcomes.

Excluded from the argument: uncited “lost revenue” estimates, unverifiable mobile-readiness percentages, vendor promises of results, UK marketing guidance used as if it were Irish law, and expired Trading Online Voucher assumptions.

Irish direct outreach must follow the relevant consent and opt-out rules. The DPC provides the local reference; no outreach was sent as part of this work. [DPC](https://www.dataprotection.ie/en/organisations/rules-electronic-and-direct-marketing).

Still to measure or document: actual delivery margin, capacity, CRM reliability for the advertised workflows, Growth user/usage allowances, support availability, cancellation/export terms, and customer willingness to pay. These are operating requirements, not reasons to replace the package structure.
