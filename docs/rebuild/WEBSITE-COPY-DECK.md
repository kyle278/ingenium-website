Ingenium is based in Carlow, Ireland. We help businesses improve how they present their services, collect enquiries and manage what happens next.

Our work brings design and technical delivery into the same conversation. That means we consider what your customers need to understand and what your team needs to do with the information they receive.

## What we care about

### Clarity before complexity

We want the website to be understandable and the CRM to be usable. We start with the task, then decide what the work needs to include.

### A scope people can follow

You should know what is included, what depends on your team and what would be additional work.

### Useful handover

The people using the work should be part of checking it. Training and the agreed support route matter alongside the build.
