Subject: We have received your enquiry

Hi [first name],

Thanks for contacting [business name] about [service].
We have received your details and will review your request.

Our next step is [explain the actual next step].
You can contact us at [phone/email] if you need to add anything.

Thanks,
[business name]

BEFORE USING
Replace every bracketed field.
Only state a response time your team can meet.
Do not confirm availability, a booking or a price that has not been agreed.
Test the contact, CRM lead, business notification and acknowledgement together.
The acknowledgement is transactional; marketing consent is a separate decision.
