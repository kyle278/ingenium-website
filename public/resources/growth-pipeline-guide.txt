BUSINESS GROWTH: PIPELINE HANDOVER GUIDE

Agree stages that match how an enquiry becomes a customer.
Example stages for discussion: New enquiry > Contacted > Quote sent > Won / Lost.

FOR EACH OPEN ENQUIRY, YOUR TEAM SHOULD KNOW:
Who is the customer?
What are they asking for?
Who owns the enquiry?
What is the current stage?
What is the next action?
When does that action need attention?

DAILY REVIEW
Check new website leads.
Add telephone and message enquiries manually unless separately integrated.
Assign an owner and update stages as the conversation moves.
Review open quotes and record the next action.

AT HANDOVER
Practise creating a contact, updating a lead and moving an opportunity.
Submit a test website enquiry and check the agreed workflow.
Record the user, record, email and execution allowances from your proposal.
Extra reminder workflows or channel integrations are additional scope.
