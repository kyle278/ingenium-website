## 1. Market and avatar

**Avatar:** An owner of a quote-led Irish service business, especially landscaping, contracting, cleaning or property services. Referrals and enquiries already happen. The owner is busy delivering work and needs either a credible website, a practical enquiry-to-quote process, or a shared operational system. Choose the package according to the problem, not an assumption that every owner needs a CRM.

| Test | Pass / Fail | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Massive pain | Conditional pass | Scattered enquiries and repeated administration have credible consequences; verify them in the customer's past examples. |
| Purchasing power | Conditional pass | Established, trading firms are stronger prospects than pre-revenue startups. Price acceptance remains untested. |
| Easy to target | Pass | Named services and locations can be identified through existing relationships, business networks and public business information. |
| Growing | Mixed | Official evidence supports a large service-business population, but not uniform growth across sectors. Do not force a “starving crowd” conclusion. |

**Niche decision:** Prioritise quote-led services, then qualify by actual workflow pain. Starter remains open to credible newly trading local businesses. Appointment-led and ecommerce work need different discovery.

Evidence and limitations: [Irish research report](IRISH-PACKAGE-VALIDATION-2026-10-29.md).

## 6. Pricing and terms

- **Price anchor:** Starter's user-supplied €795 standard setup, reduced to €495 during the fixed promotion. Higher tiers anchor to a customer's own process costs, established during discovery.
- **Total stated value:** No artificial monetary total asserted. Standalone values and a 10× ratio cannot be substantiated from supplied evidence.
- **Price:** Starter €495 promotional / €795 standard + €50/month; Growth from €1,500 + from €99/month indicative; CRM from €3,000 + from €199/month indicative.
- **Why this price:** Scope provides a testable base. Competitive desk research supports plausibility, not profitability. Cost delivery before treating indicative prices as universal fixed quotes.
- **Terms:** Starter 50/50; Growth 60/40; CRM 50/30/20. Final review/acceptance precedes final balance and launch/handover. Subscriptions begin at launch unless an existing agreement says otherwise.
- **Buyer responsibilities:** Supply accurate material and access, consolidate feedback, clean the agreed import, nominate an approver and participate in training.
- **Tiering:** Online presence → enquiry management → operational process implementation. No invented premium capacity limit.

## 9. Guarantee

**Name:** Scope First, Review Before Handover.
**Statement:** “If the agreed implementation does not pass its agreed acceptance checks, we address the in-scope defects before the final acceptance milestone.”
**Type:** Conditional service/acceptance commitment, not a revenue guarantee.
**Conditions:** Written scope, accurate supplied inputs, access, timely feedback, and changes handled as additional scope.
**Why this one:** It addresses the buyer's fear of paying for something unclear or unusable while preserving objective boundaries.

CRM adds the user's existing **14-day defect warranty after handover**. It covers agreed functionality, not new requests. No new refund, free-month delivery credit or unlimited correction period has been published. Any stronger guarantee requires a costed remedy and written terms before launch.

## 11. The offer in one paragraph (as the prospect would read it)

Run a service business in Ireland? Start with what needs to work better. Starter Website gives customers a clear place to check your work and get in touch, with a five-working-day target once your material is ready. Business Growth connects up to five website pages to a simple CRM and an automated first response, so your team can see what happens next. CRM & Automation brings established customer processes into one shared system, with agreed workflows, training and acceptance checks. See your setup and monthly costs upfront, review a written scope before committing and approve the agreed work before final handover. No VAT charged. The Starter setup promotion ends at the published deadline; the higher packages have indicative starting prices.
